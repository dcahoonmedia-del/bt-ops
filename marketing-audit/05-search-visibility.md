## 2. Maps / GBP competitive physics

Google Business Profile proximity + reviews + category + activity dominate “near me.”

| Market | Likely pack gravity | B&T handicap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jacksonville / Camp Lejeune | Modern (College St), May (Commerce Rd), Eastline | B&T pin is **Holly Ridge**, ~20+ minutes south |
| Holly Ridge / Surf City / Topsail | B&T should be native | Must keep photos, posts, categories, Q&A tight |
| Sneads Ferry | Mixed Onslow | Compete with Jones, Eastline, Diamond |
| Hampstead | Diamond branch, Cape Fear, Riggs | B&T is nearby HQ — winnable |
| Wilmington / Wrightsville | ILM-based companies | Distance + review volume |
| Swansboro / Cape Carteret | May, Orkin New Bern, Eastline | Distance + Carteret incumbents |

**Do not spend the year building 40 city pages expecting Maps wins in Jacksonville.** City pages help **organic** and ads landing; Maps in JAX requires a **JAX-area presence strategy** (service-area vs second office vs tech parking) — operations decision, not a blog decision.

## 6. Practical visibility conclusion

1. **B&T can win organic** on service + city pages it already has if titles are de-stuffed and internals point at Jacksonville/Wilmington/Surf City money pages.  
2. **B&T cannot out-Modern Modern on Jacksonville Maps** with blog posts.

4. **WDIR/VA** is an underserved B&T page vs Eastline/May.  
5. **Paid** is how Aptive/Orkin/Hawx appear in B&T’s own towns; ignoring it is a choice, not a default.
