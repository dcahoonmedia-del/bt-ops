## 2. How the listing looks to a homeowner comparing 3 pins

**Strengths**

- 4.9 is **elite**. Most shoppers stop reading stars and start reading **count**.  
- 271–285 is **credible** (not 12 reviews) but **not dominant** next to Modern’s 2,300.  
- Tenure and named techs (Josh, Michael/Michel) show up in review text — that is how locals pick.  
- Price mentions in reviews (“great prices,” “great value,” 17–20 year customers) support the $45 story.

**Weaknesses vs a Jacksonville shopper**

- Pin is Holly Ridge. For “pest control near me” inside 28546, Google often prefers College St / Commerce Rd companies.  
- Hours mismatch looks sloppy if GBP says 9–6 and the website says 8–5.  
- Surf City vs Holly Ridge city name confuses NAP.  
- If Posts are empty (unverified), the listing looks dormant next to nationals who auto-post coupons.  
- Yelp 4.0/6 including a 2022 crime allegation (see reviews file) can appear in the knowledge panel ecosystem even if Google is 4.9.

**Weaknesses vs a Surf City / Topsail shopper**

- This is B&T’s **home field**. A complete photo set (trucks, techs in branded shirts, crawl spaces, beach houses, bait stations, before/after fire ant mounds) should make this the obvious tap. If photos are generic stock, that is a self-inflicted wound.
