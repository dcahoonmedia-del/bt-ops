## 4. Where B&T is cheaper / more expensive / better value

**Cheaper (advertised apples-to-apples):**

- vs Mosquito Authority $89–$149/mo: PestGuard $45 **includes mosquitoes**.  
- vs Diamond $269 initial (if B&T setup after $75 off is below that — **unknown until setup is published**).  
- vs Aptive ~$70/mo reported: PestGuard $45.

**More expensive (monthlyized):**

- vs Cape Fear $85/quarter if the customer only wants basic crawling insects. Cape Fear’s list is narrower (no advertised mosquito/fire-ant inclusion).  
- vs a $29 B&T Basic customer who actually has mosquitoes — they bought the wrong SKU; that’s a sales problem, not a price problem.

**Better value even if not cheapest:**

- PestGuard $540/year for 6 visits + yard + mosquitoes + fire ants + callback guarantee is the coastal product Cape Fear’s $340/year basic quarterly is not.  
- Plus $708/year vs PTC $700/year: similar money; B&T claims **liquid + bait + borate**, PTC claims **free Trelona**. Feature fight, not price fight.  
- Heroes $25 **stackable** vs Mosquito Joe 5% non-stackable.

**Unknown / must not claim:**

- Whether B&T’s unpublished initial fee makes year-1 cash more or less than Diamond $269 or Orkin $200–$350. **Publish or quote-tool this.**

## 5. Offer strategy assessment

| Offer | Attractiveness | Clarity | Recurring revenue | Risk of cheap customers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $75 off Basic setup | Medium | **Poor** (headline vs Basic-only body) | Pulls people onto the **weakest** plan | Medium |
| $100 off Plus | High | Good | Best LTV (pest+termite) | Low |
| $250 crawl | High for the right job | Good | Ancillary ticket | Low |
| Free $225 exterior w/ termite | High | Good | Termite LTV | Low |
| $25 heroes stackable | High near Lejeune | Good | Recurring | Low |
| 10% wildlife | Medium | OK | Job ticket | Low |
| $10–$50 referral | Weak vs competitors | Clear | Recurring if PestGuard | Low |

**Should not copy:** Mosquito Joe 57% off (trains discount addiction); Aptive 12-month clawback (review poison); Hawx $150 off unless CAC is measured; Cape Fear $85/quarter as B&T’s hero (destroys mosquito-inclusion story).

**Should lean into:** $45 all-in vs $89 mosquito franchise; $100 off Plus; heroes stacked with Plus; rental/beach-house annual prepay (not currently offered).
