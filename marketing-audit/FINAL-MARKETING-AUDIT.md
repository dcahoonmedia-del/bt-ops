## 1. Executive summary

B&T is a **44-year, family-owned, Holly Ridge operator** with a **4.9 Google rating**, a **named recurring product (PestGuard)**, and the most honest public price list in the coastal strip: **$29 / $45 / $59 per month**. The $45 plan includes mosquitoes, fire ants, fleas, ticks, and stinging insects — pests Mosquito Authority Jacksonville sells at **$89–$149/month**. That is a real, copyable-but-not-yet-copied commercial advantage.

B&T is **not** the default when a Jacksonville or Wilmington homeowner opens Google Maps. **Modern Exterminating** (~4.8 stars, **2,300+ reviews**, in-town since 1952) owns Jacksonville trust-at-a-glance. **Eastline** and **May** own military/WDIR language. **Mosquito franchises** own mosquito queries. **Aptive / Orkin / Terminix / Hawx** own paid attention. **Diamond Pest Pro** ($269 start, $299/year termite, 780+ reviews, Hampstead branch) is the fast-growing regional in B&T’s own driveway.

B&T **wins** Holly Ridge–Topsail–Sneads Ferry shoppers who find the website or already know the name. B&T **loses** Maps-first Jacksonville ants jobs, VA closing WDIRs, and “mosquito control” searches. The five highest-leverage moves in 90 days are packaging (homepage), hygiene (NAP/hours), two service pages (fire ants + mosquito comparison), a Camp Lejeune/WDIR page, and tightly geo-fenced paid search — not a 40-city SEO mill and not matching national $150 coupons.
