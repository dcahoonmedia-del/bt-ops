## 5. CRITICAL FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS

### 🚨 HIGH PRIORITY ISSUES

#### 1. Website Form Broken (Since May 2026)
**Evidence:**
- Zero webform leads from btpestcontrol.com in July 2026 (Coalmarch data)
- Client email confirmed "WDIR website form broke in May 2026"
- Forms still arriving from coalmarchleads.com (alternate landing page)

**Impact:** Loss of direct website conversions; visitors hitting broken form may bounce

**Recommendation:**
- **Immediate:** Test & fix btpestcontrol.com contact form
- Verify form submissions are reaching correct email/CRM
- Implement form monitoring/alerts to detect future breakage

#### 2. CPL Efficiency Declining
**Evidence:**
- YTD CPL up 104% vs 2025 despite 120% spend increase
- Only 8% lead volume growth with doubled spend
- Paid channel CPL ($61) significantly higher than organic ($46.40)

**Possible Causes:**
- Increased competition driving up CPCs
- Audience saturation (same targeting over extended period)
- Ad fatigue or declining ad relevance
- Budget allocation inefficiency between LSA vs PPC

**Recommendation:**
- **Audit Campaign Settings:** Review Google Ads (LSA & PPC) targeting, bidding, keyword quality scores
- **Test New Audiences:** Expand or refine geographic/demographic targeting
- **Ad Creative Refresh:** Update ad copy, test new angles, refresh LSA business photos
- **Channel Reallocation:** Consider shifting more budget to organic (lower CPL) or test new channels

#### 3. GBP Description Mentions "Lawn Care"
**Evidence:** Current description: "Firm providing termite, bug, mosquito and rodent control, along with lawn care services."

**Impact:**
- May attract irrelevant inquiries for lawn mowing/landscaping
- Dilutes core pest control brand positioning
- Could impact LSA qualification/relevance

**Recommendation:**
- **Update GBP Description:** Remove "lawn care" unless it's a genuine service offering
- Focus on core pest control services: termite, mosquito, rodent, bed bug, etc.
- Add specific value props: "Family-friendly," "44+ years experience," "Eastern NC's choice since 1982"

#### 4. Services List Includes Irrelevant Items
**Evidence:** Prior audit noted services include "Home Construction"

**Impact:** Confuses potential customers, may hurt LSA/GBP relevance signals

**Recommendation:**
- Audit entire GBP services list
- Remove any non-pest-control services
- Add missing pest control services if applicable

### ⚠️ MEDIUM PRIORITY OBSERVATIONS

#### 5. Lead Type Shift: Messages Up, Calls Down
**Evidence:**
- Message leads +92% (last 30d)
- Call leads -22% (last 30d)

**Possible Causes:**
- LSA increasingly driving messaging vs calls
- Customer preference shifting toward asynchronous communication
- Call button visibility or ease-of-use issue on mobile

**Recommendation:**
- **Monitor Trend:** Track whether this continues or stabilizes
- **Message Response Process:** Ensure swift response times to GBP messages (LSA scoring factor)
- **Test Call CTA Prominence:** Make phone number more visible on website/GBP

#### 6. Budget Cut Impact on Lead Volume
**Evidence:** August budget cut from $4,600 to $2,000 (technician out)

**Impact:** August leads will be significantly lower; may lose market share to competitors

**Recommendation:**
- Resume full budget when technician returns
- Consider minimum "floor" budget to maintain brand presence even during staff shortages
- Implement campaign scheduling to maintain coverage without exceeding budget

#### 7. Seasonal Performance Pattern
**Evidence:** July peak (156 leads, $45.78 CPL best), slower Q1

**Recommendation:**
- Plan Q2/Q3 budget increases for seasonal demand
- Pre-emptive Q1 campaigns (termite swarms, early spring pests)
- Adjust bidding strategies by season
