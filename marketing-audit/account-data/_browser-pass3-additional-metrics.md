## 6. CONSOLIDATED FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Critical Issues Identified:
1. **Website Form Breakage (May 2026)** - Confirmed by zero webform leads across all months. Forms arriving from coalmarchleads.com suggest Coalmarch is using their own landing pages.
2. **Standard Google Ads Account Dormant** - 173 campaigns paused, $0 spend in last 90 days. Suggests strategic shift to LSA-focused approach.
3. **GBP Description Misalignment** - Profile mentions "lawn care services" which may confuse customers or attract irrelevant inquiries.
4. **GBP Service Category Cleanup Needed** - "Home Construction" appears as a service category (junk data).

### Performance Trends:
1. **Lead Volume Stable** - 683 leads over 13 months (~52.5/month average)
2. **CPL Rising** - From $41-46 (mid-2025) to $50-60 (early 2026), now $46-54 (Q2 2026)
3. **Message Leads Growing** - 92% increase in recent period, offsetting declining call volume
4. **Seasonal Patterns** - Lower spend/leads in winter (Dec-Feb), higher in summer (Jun-Aug)
5. **Budget Adjustment in August 2026** - User noted paid budget cut from $4,600 to $2,000 because a technician is out

### Recommended Next Steps:
1. **Fix Website Forms** - Priority #1, currently all leads via phone/message only
2. **Review LSA Strategy** - Since LSA appears to be primary channel, conduct deep dive on LSA account performance, responsiveness, and dispute management
3. **Clean Up GBP** - Remove "lawn care" from description, remove "Home Construction" from services
4. **Evaluate Standard Search Revival** - Consider whether any of the 173 paused campaigns should be reactivated based on historical performance
5. **Implement Call Tracking** - Ensure all call sources (LSA, GBP, organic) are properly tracked in CallTrackingMetrics
