## 3. ISSUES & OPPORTUNITIES

### Conversion Issues:
1. **Two phone numbers** - May confuse customers. Website shows 910-356-9966 but official is 910-329-1337
2. **Form is comprehensive but long** - Many fields may reduce completion
3. **No live chat widget observed** - Missing real-time engagement option
4. **No text-us button** - Despite SMS opt-in checkbox, no prominent text CTA
5. **Pricing not shown** - No transparent pricing on homepage (could be intentional for lead gen)
6. **"MY ACCOUNT" button** - Prominent but may not be relevant for first-time visitors

### Missing Elements:
- No chat widget visible
- No SMS/text click-to-text button
- No instant quote calculator
- No pricing transparency (common in pest control, but worth noting)
- No video testimonials (only text reviews)
- No before/after photos

### Strengths:
- Professional, clean design
- Strong trust signals
- Clear service area
- Good review integration
- Mobile-responsive (observed)
- Fast load time
- Multiple CTAs without being overwhelming
- Strong local SEO signals (city names, NC focus)
