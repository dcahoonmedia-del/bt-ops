Rodent Control in Wilmington, NC

In Wilmington, rodent control is about more than traps; it’s about protecting the structure of your home. With riverfront areas, crawl spaces, and year-round moisture, homes here attract roof rats and Norway rats looking for shelter inside attics, walls, and garages. Quick fixes don’t hold up in these conditions, which is why effective rodent control starts with exclusion.

The B&T Rodent Defense Process:

  • Professional evaluation: A detailed inspection of the entire structure to identify entry points as small as a dime.
  • Permanent exclusion: Exterior sealing using professional-grade materials to block access points and stop rodents from getting back inside.
  • Targeted interior removal: Safe mechanical trapping and customized baiting plans to eliminate active rodents already in the home.
  • Clean and sanitize options: Removal of nesting materials and treatment of scent trails to reduce health risks and discourage future activity.

Secure your Wilmington home with a rodent control process built for long-term protection, not temporary fixes!

Crawl Space Moisture Control in Wilmington, NC

In Wilmington, high groundwater and constant humidity allow moisture to build up beneath homes year-round. That moisture leads to wood rot, mold growth, and increased termite activity if left untreated. B&T Pest Control provides crawl space moisture control systems designed specifically for Wilmington conditions. Our Crawl Space Moisture Control Process in Wilmington includes: 

  • Comprehensive inspection to identify moisture sources, airflow issues, and areas of active or potential damage.
  • Reinforced vapor barrier installation to block ground moisture and reduce humidity rising into the home.
  • Sealing of vents and penetrations to limit outside moisture and unconditioned air from entering the crawl space.
  • Industrial-grade dehumidifier installation with dedicated drainage to maintain consistent humidity control.
  • Ongoing moisture stabilization that helps protect structural wood, reduce mold conditions, and limit pest activity.
