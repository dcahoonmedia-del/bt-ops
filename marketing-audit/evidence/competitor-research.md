# B&T Pest Control competitor database

**Subject:** B&T Pest Control (Holly Ridge / Eastern NC; [btpestcontrol.com](https://www.btpestcontrol.com/))  
**Research date:** 2026-08-23  
**Method:** Public web sources only (company sites, NCPMA, chambers, BBB, Yelp, Angi, review aggregators, franchise location pages). **No lead forms submitted. No companies contacted as a fake customer.**  
**Unverified fields:** marked **Unknown**.  
**Pricing rule:** advertised = published on the company’s own site; customer-reported = third-party reviews/directories. Do not treat aggregator “typical cost” ranges as this company’s price.

B&T’s advertised home-market footprint (for overlap analysis): Jacksonville, Wilmington, Surf City, Holly Ridge, Hampstead, Sneads Ferry, Swansboro, Richlands, Hubert, Piney Green, Half Moon, Verona, Burgaw, Rocky Point, Ogden, Porters Neck, Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach, Cape Carteret, Newport, Maple Hill, Topsail, North Topsail Beach, Kure Beach, Maysville, Cedar Point, plus Onslow, Pender, New Hanover, and parts of Carteret/Jones. Source: B&T city pages and [areas-we-service](https://www.btpestcontrol.com/) captures in this repo, accessed 2026-08-23.

## How to read this file

- **Scale:** National (multi-state brand/franchise), Regional (multi-market NC/SE, not a national household name), Local (one-county or few-county operator).
- **Google ratings:** Prefer location-specific counts. Many national brands publish *company-wide* stars. Aggregators (Birdeye, ProsGrade, PestControlReviews) mix Google/Facebook/other; labeled as such.
- **Social follower counts:** Almost never published on public pages without logging in. Marked Unknown unless a public count was visible.
- **Overlap:** “Direct” = advertises cities B&T names. “Adjacent” = counties B&T may reach (Carteret, Jones, Brunswick, Duplin, Craven) that steal share at the edges.

A companion machine-readable table is in [`competitors-draft.csv`](./competitors-draft.csv).

## Most important threats (ranked)

These are the competitors most likely to take B&T customers, not merely the ones with the biggest national brand.

1. **Modern Exterminating (Jacksonville)** — 74-year Onslow incumbent, NCPMA + chamber + builder-association member, ~2,300 review volume, full termite/moisture/WDIR stack, covers every Onslow/Carteret/Pender/Jones town B&T names. Highest “default local” risk in Jacksonville / Camp Lejeune.
2. **Jay Taylor Exterminating (Wilmington)** — 85-year fourth-generation Cape Fear brand, Sentricon Always Active, A+ BBB, high review volume, advertised Onslow + Pender coverage. Highest “default local” risk in Wilmington / beach towns.
3. **Aptive (Wilmington branch)** — National door-to-door / inbound machine that *lists every B&T city* (Holly Ridge, Jacksonville, Surf-adjacent towns, Swansboro, Maysville, Newport, etc.) from one Wilmington office. Long sales hours (M–F to 10pm). Steals first-time homeowners and military PCS moves.
4. **Bug-N-A-Rug** — Dual-office (Wilmington + Cedar Point) same-day operator with 4.9-star / 3,300+ review claim, heat bed bugs, Trelona, crawl encapsulation, mosquito FOGO, 5% prepay and BOGO mosquito promos running through 08/31/2026. Direct Topsail–Crystal Coast overlap.
5. **Diamond Pest Pro** — Newest scaled local (2020) with a **Hampstead branch** sitting in B&T’s corridor, 4.9 / 780+ Google (company-wide), public prices, 100% callback guarantee, HOA/property-management packaging. Digital-native competitor.
6. **Mosquito Joe + Mosquito Authority + Mosquito Squad** — Three Neighborly/specialty brands that own “yard is unusable” demand. B&T includes mosquitoes in PestGuard at $45/mo; these specialists advertise 21-day cycles, event sprays, and (Authority) $89–$149/mo bundles. High seasonal share risk.
7. **Eastline Pest Management** — QualityPro regional from New Bern that explicitly targets Camp Lejeune military families, VA WDIR-100, PCS timelines, Termidor, moisture, and lists Jacksonville, Swansboro, Hubert, Holly Ridge, Sneads Ferry, Maysville, Piney Green.
8. **Pest & Termite Consultants** — Advertises **$175/quarter** pest + free Trelona install (claimed $700 value) and 4.9 / 840+ Google. Direct Sneads Ferry / Jacksonville / Holly Ridge / Surf City pages. Transparent program pricing undercuts “call for quote” locals.
9. **Hawx** — National-style subscription (90-day) from a Wilmington office, 4.6 / 1,987 Google, same/next-day, free re-service warranty. Aggressive Jacksonville landing pages.
10. **Orkin Branch 342 + Terminix Wilmington + Massey Wilmington** — Brand, 24/7 or next-day, commercial accounts, Sentricon/Termidor, money-back guarantees. Orkin lists Holly Ridge, Sneads Ferry, Burgaw, Hampstead, Carolina Beach. Terminix lists Holly Ridge, Surf City, Wrightsville, Burgaw.

Honorable mentions that punch above size: **May Exterminating** (60+ years, $60 military move-out spray), **Pestco** (price-match, 50+ years, Onslow expansion), **Rid-A-Pest** (same-day from Morehead, 4.9 / 1,925), **G’s Pest Control** (Sneads Ferry/Holly Ridge/NTB specialist in B&T’s driveway), **Port City Pest** (wildlife + alligator differentiator in Wilmington).

- **Modern / May / Jay Taylor** win on tenure + realtor/WDIR muscle, not on web design.
