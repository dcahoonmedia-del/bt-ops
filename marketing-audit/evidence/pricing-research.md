# Eastern NC pest control pricing research

**Date observed:** 2026-08-23  
**Market:** Jacksonville, Wilmington, Surf City, Hampstead, Swansboro, Holly Ridge, Sneads Ferry, Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach; Onslow, Pender, and New Hanover counties.  
**Method:** Public web pages, FAQs, coupon/offer pages, search snippets, third-party review aggregators, and public ad-library mirrors. **No quote forms submitted. No companies called.**  
**Companion files:** `pricing-draft.csv`, `ads-research.md`

Price-type labels used throughout:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Verified advertised | Dollar amount or offer copied from the company’s own live page |
| Starting price | Company labels it “starting at” or equivalent |
| Promotion | Discount, code, or limited-time offer |
| Customer-reported | Review or discussion comment, not a company rate card |
| Estimated market range | Aggregator or industry guide, not a specific company quote |

Confidence: **High** = seen on the company site; **Medium** = reputable third-party quoting a company, or a review with a number; **Low** = market range, lead-gen page, or offer that may be geo-gated / stale.

## 6. Offer-pattern synthesis (what B&T is up against)

1. **B&T is unusually transparent.** In this DMA, only Cape Fear ($85/qtr), PTC ($175/qtr), Diamond ($269 start / $299 termite year), and Mosquito Authority ($89 / $149 / $39) publish recurring prices. Nationals hide ZIP-quoted prices behind address tools that were **not** used (per instructions).

2. **Monthlyized vs per-visit framing.** B&T, Hawx, Aptive, Terminix, and Mosquito Authority sell **monthly**. Cape Fear and PTC sell **quarterly visit prices**. $85/quarter looks cheaper than $45/month until annualized ($340 vs $540). B&T PestGuard’s extra cost buys bi-monthly cadence plus advertised mosquito/tick/fire-ant inclusion.

3. **Initial-fee discount is the default national CTA.** Orkin $75 (APPLY75), Terminix $50–$75, Massey $50, Hawx $150, Clegg’s $39, Mosquito Joe $25 first treatment, Aptive $200 off a $399 list. B&T’s **$75 off setup** is in the same band as Orkin/Terminix, **below** Hawx’s $150 headline.

4. **Pest + termite bundles are the local fight.** B&T Plus $59/mo vs PTC Plus $175/qtr (~$58/mo) with free Trelona. Terminix Triad analog $102/mo pest+termite (not this DMA). Diamond $299/year termite-only is a cheap bond, not a combined plan.

5. **Mosquito is either included or a separate franchise SKU.** B&T includes it in PestGuard. Mosquito Joe and Mosquito Authority sell it as the hero product (Joe: 57% off “Home Pest Defense with Mosquito”; Authority: $89 bundle). Massey, Hawx, Orkin, Terminix sell mosquito as add-on/seasonal.

6. **Hero discounts** are common near Camp Lejeune. B&T’s $25 is a flat dollar off **and stackable**. Clegg’s 15% is richer on a large ticket. Mosquito Joe 5% is weaker and **not** combinable.

7. **Referral economics.** B&T $10–$50 both sides. Mosquito Joe $50/$50 up to $500. Economy $50 + $500 drawing. Aptive $50–$100. B&T’s PestGuard referral ($20) is light for a monthly plan.

8. **Contracts.** Aptive: 12-month + discount clawback. Hawx TOH quote: repay discount on cancel. PTC: “no long-term contracts.” Mosquito Authority Jacksonville: “no commitments.” B&T pages emphasize guarantee, not term length (term not verified here).

9. **Price hiding is the national playbook.** Orkin, Terminix, Aptive, Hawx, Massey, Arrow, Port City, Jones, Canady, Economy require inspection or ZIP before a number. B&T’s public $29/$45/$59 is a positioning choice, not industry standard.
