## 4. What customers love

1. **A named technician who acts like a neighbor, not a spray-and-go route.** Josh McLamb / Joshua and Michael / Michel Arnold dominate recent 5-stars. People write the *person*, then the company.
2. **Thoroughness beyond the obvious.** Ant mounds across a large yard; closets for spiders; finding issues the homeowner missed; explaining before leaving.
3. **Speed vs. the last company.** Same-day (Renee S.); weekend return call (Nicole P.); “quicker than expected” (Brantley); competitor charged 3× and never treated inside (Thomas H.).
4. **Results on visible coastal pests.** Fire ants, ants, spiders, black widow, general “no bugs.”
5. **Longevity / “they’ve had my beach house for years.”** 17–20 year customers are testimonial gold for Topsail / military / second-home market.
6. **Local vs. national framing.** Kate M. researched options and chose local; Pam C. switched companies; Thomas H. escaped a bad prior vendor.
